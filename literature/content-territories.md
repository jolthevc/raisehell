# Content Territories | Where the Truths Live

Territories are **subjects**, not emotions or formulas. Mix them with emotional-engineering.md and post-archetypes.md.

## 1. Agency and self-deception
Avoidance disguised as preparation; the stories we tell to keep options open; bargaining with tomorrow; the comforting lie of "when I am ready." Avoid shaming people who face actual constraints.

## 2. Ambition and useful obsession
Wanting more, the embarrassment of having big dreams, competitive appetite, starting late, making things, betting on yourself. Never equate human worth with money, status, productivity, or prestige.

## 3. Discipline and self-trust
Kept promises, repeated small choices, the dignity of practice, keeping going when no one is watching. Not every problem is a willpower deficit.

## 4. Confidence and social fear
Being perceived, rejection, awkwardness, taking risks, outgrowing imaginary audiences, self-respect without arrogance, going where you are wanted.

## 5. Love and relationships
Missing people, leaving, staying, telling the truth, letting yourself be known, the courage of affection, the relief of mutual care. Avoid cynical gender-war frames, manipulation, and crude dating rules.

## 6. Family and friendship
The unasked question, parents as human beings, long friendships, being a good friend, saying thank you, growing apart without contempt. Never presume everyone has safe family relationships.

## 7. Time, aging, and mortality
Finite summers, photographs, ordinary days that become memories, being alive now, the difference between planning and living. Never turn grief into a guilt funnel.

## 8. Failure and recovery
Embarrassment, disappointment, imperfection, reinvention, living after mistakes, being a beginner, getting back up. No guaranteed victory narrative.

## 9. Pleasure and audacity
Laughing too hard, doing things badly for fun, taking the trip, asking the question, dancing, celebrating a small win. Living is not only overcoming adversity.

## 10. Identity and reinvention
The self others expect, the self you are allowed to become, choosing new standards, leaving old rooms. No blank-check abandonment of dependents or real obligations.

## 11. Solitude and comparison
Being alone without being unloved, comparison spirals, external validation, status games, envy, a quieter relationship with your own mind.

## 12. Hard conversations
Apologies, boundaries, asking for help, ending ambiguity, owning a mistake, saying the hard thing kindly.

## Finding better ideas
For each territory, collect **real behaviors**, not broad themes. Examples: making a joke about a dream before anyone else can criticize it; checking whether someone watched your story; rehearsing an apology but never delivering it; looking at old photos and realizing the people were younger than you remember.

## Diversity and restraint
Don't create a rigid quota per category. Use the territories to spot editorial repetition and broaden the account. A powerful work/fear post is not forbidden because the previous post mentioned work. But twenty near-identical discipline posts erase the brand's emotional range.

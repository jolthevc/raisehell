# Language and Style | Blood Moving, Words Landing

## Spoken-language power
Write like a person with real conviction who cares enormously. Clear contemporary English. Contractions. Direct address. Concrete, emphatic verbs. Not a therapist, academic, corporate account, cryptic poet, Hollywood trailer voice, or clichéd motivational guru.

**The essential difference:** the reader should feel *called forward*, not *called out and left there*.

## Default size and flow
Prefer **2 or 3 compact paragraphs**:
1. A high-impact opening that interrupts autopilot.
2. A middle that personally builds possibility, pressure, excitement or belief.
3. An ending with an undeniable push, challenge or affirmation.

Often paragraphs are 1-4 sentences. Use a rhythm of conversational and punchy sentences. A longer passage is fine only if it accelerates rather than digresses. A shorter piece is fine if it *really* lands. The standard isn't exactly three paragraphs; it's **earned crescendo**.

## Profanity is part of the house vocabulary
**Use it frequently when effective.** Fuck, fucking, shit, damn and hell belong naturally in RAISE HELL. A curse can unlock the voice, turn polite hesitation into fire, make an idea funny or put weight behind a call to action.

Do not force a swear-word quota, remove curses to sound respectable, use slurs, or inject random "fucking" into otherwise lifeless sentences. Some posts may swear more than once; a rare quiet post may not swear. Vary placement and sound.

## Rhetorical devices that fit
- Questions with actual bite: audacious, urgent, sometimes profane.
- Accumulation: "The one where... The one where..." creates accelerating belief.
- Direct imperatives: "Take it. Put your name on it." can be incredible when earned.
- Repetition: increase pressure, don't simply rephrase the same sentence.
- Contrasts: imagining disaster versus imagining success; being knocked down versus being finished.
- Affection and comedy: humanizing idioms, a grin before the final challenge.
- Permission with muscle: wanting an extraordinary life, joy, love, ambition.
- Callbacks: the ending can pay off the opening, but it must **move**.

Use all selectively. These are techniques, not a mandated script for every post.

## Endings
The closing needs to **hit harder than the observation that began it**. Deliver a command, courageous question, emphatic belief, irresistible invitation or forward-moving image. Prefer "What will you do?" energy over "Isn't that interesting?" energy.

Avoid mechanically ending every post with "Get up," "Take the shot," "Do it anyway," or "Let's go." Repeated motivational phrasing can go stale. Invent different, specific launches.

## What to avoid
- Gentle psychological explanations and counseling language as the default.
- Disconnected philosophical aphorisms that stop before the emotional payoff.
- "You're enough" reassurance that dissolves into passivity.
- Cynical shame masquerading as accountability.
- Abstract status nouns: alpha, empire, king, wolf, warrior, greatness, grindset.
- Fake antagonists or guarantees: "They all doubt you," "You'll definitely succeed."
- Filler like "Let that sink in" and manufactured mic-drop pauses.
- Copying distinctive Gold setups, clauses, punchlines or memorable turns.

## Mechanics
Avoid em dashes. Use periods, commas, question marks, line breaks and occasional colons. Avoid hashtags and emoji in publishable copy. Keep text readable on a black Instagram post. Do not invent specific statistics, personal testimony or psychological certainty.

## Read-aloud litmus
Would this sound natural in a high-stakes pep talk given by someone who believes in you? **Did the final line raise your energy?** If not, rewrite the landing before fussing over decoration.

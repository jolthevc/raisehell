# RAISE HELL

**A little fire for your day.** An Instagram account for sharp, fearless, memorable short sayings.

## Start here: current creative workflow

### [RAISE_HELL_DAILY.md — canonical daily handoff](RAISE_HELL_DAILY.md)

**As of October 15, 2026, writing is MANUAL in ChatGPT, not automated through n8n.** Each morning, the owner can say **"RAISE HELL. Give me five."** ChatGPT returns five original sayings that are **ideally 15–35 words (strictly under 60) and at most two paragraphs**. The user selects/revises, places copy as white text on a black background, screenshots/exports, adds music and schedules/posts manually.

This is **fire, not motivational mini essays**. The owner's latest direction: **blunt, unpolished, forceful commands**, captured verbatim in `RAISE_HELL_DAILY.md`: “Get the fuck up,” “You failed? Great,” “Again and again and again.” **The ultimate benchmark is ULTRA FIRE:** reader gets absolutely fired up, emotionally charged and ready to attack the day. **Conviction, urgency and repetition beat clever literary similes, analogies, metaphors or long speeches.** “Ready to go to war” refers to drive and determination, not literal violence. Direct, urgent, demanding and fiercely on the reader's side, without campy sentiment or alpha-status posing. Brief, bold, irreverent and spoken like a real person, not a generic self-help speaker. **Every piece ends with a hard, urgent push to action, never a soft takeaway.** The owner chooses what is publishable. **Default post caption: `RAISE HELL.`**

For any new ChatGPT session, **read [the complete daily handoff](RAISE_HELL_DAILY.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)**, then write the five sayings. The handoff includes original short-form demonstration examples, the history of rejected formats, owner feedback, and a place to retain future expressly approved copy. A new chat can be restored by providing that one URL.

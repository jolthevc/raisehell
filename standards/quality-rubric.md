# Quality Rubric | Would This Get Your Blood Going?

## The benchmark
The seven owner-approved [Editorial Gold](editorial-gold.md) posts are the primary positive benchmark. The mission is not "interesting literature." It is **exceptionally written, forceful, life-affirming motivation that moves people into action, belief, love or joy**.

Scores are editorial judgments, not engagement predictions. Real published performance belongs to [Performance Gold](gold-standards.md).

## Hard veto
Apply [anti-patterns.md](anti-patterns.md) first. No score can make contempt, plagiarism, fabrication or manipulative harm acceptable.

## Six dimensions (1 to 5 each)
| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Emotional ignition** | inert | some feeling | unmistakable surge of courage, optimism, determination, love or joy |
| **Forward momentum and landing** | flat ending | a serviceable close | the ending *promotes* the reader into action or powerful belief |
| **Voice and conviction** | generic or therapeutic | energetic but interchangeable | an unmistakable, profane-when-earned RAISE HELL speaker |
| **Human truth and stakes** | hollow or false | relatable | credible, personally felt tension, desire or possibility |
| **Writing and rhythm** | clumsy, repetitive | readable | gripping opening, accumulating middle, charged ending |
| **Shareability and distinctiveness** | forgettable | plausibly shareable | a person wants to save it or send it to someone who needs it |

A central theme can be familiar. **Do not score originality as if every post must invent a never-before-seen philosophical insight.** Reward original *delivery and emotional force*, not obscurity.

## Initial bar
- **26-30:** strong candidate for human review.
- **25:** meets the initial target if the piece feels extraordinary as a whole.
- **20-24:** revise, especially the emotional arc and ending.
- **Below 20:** rebuild or reject.

**Non-numeric gate:** If **Emotional ignition** or **Forward momentum and landing** scores under 3/5, a post is normally not a publishable RAISE HELL candidate. Do not game scores to fill a batch.

## Read-aloud challenge
- Did the first line make me stop?
- Is the speaker on the reader's side, not tearing them down?
- Did the second paragraph build greater stakes, possibility or excitement?
- Would I feel more alive, hopeful, brave, hungry or eager to love after reading?
- Is the final paragraph the strongest, or did the energy leak out?
- Does profanity add human conviction rather than fake toughness?
- Does it compare to the actual Gold writing, without borrowing it?
- Would it work on the simple black square with no music?
- Is the idea differentiated enough from recent drafts?
- Has the owner approved publication?

## Batch evaluation
A ten-post batch can have similar direct address, profanity and 2-3 paragraph rhythm. **That is a brand signature, not a failure.** Require multiple truly strong messages and enough emotional range: especially optimism, challenge, courage, resilience, life enjoyment and love.

Do not impose exact register quotas or structural quotas. Reject a pile of safe psychological reflections, repeated "What if it works?" paraphrases, empty grind commands, or underpowered endings.

If the weakest pieces would be embarrassing beside Editorial Gold, generate better ones. Returning fewer exceptional candidates is preferable to making mediocre writing look approved.

## States
**REJECT** (hard veto or irreparable draft), **REVISE** (strong kernel, weak delivery), **CANDIDATE** (human selection required), **APPROVED** (explicit human approval to publish), **PUBLISHED** (actually posted), **EDITORIAL GOLD** (owner approves *as a writing benchmark*), **PERFORMANCE GOLD** (published and selected with real observed data).

**Editorial Gold status does not itself authorize posting or assert performance.** Never fabricate approval, analytics or breakout success.

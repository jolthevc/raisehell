# Visual Identity and Profile | Initial Lock

This document records the agreed initial direction, not a fully engineered design system.

## Core profile
- **Display name:** RAISE HELL
- **Handle direction:** @raisehelldaily, verify availability before setting
- **Tagline:** A little fire for your day.
- **Bio direction:** A little fire for your day. / One shot at being alive. Don't sleepwalk through it.
- **Symbol:** one lit matchstick against black
- **Logo mood:** simple, bold, rebellious, hand-touched rather than glossy

## Profile icon
Single off-white match, diagonal and readable, with a vivid red-orange flame. Black background. No text, mascot, skull, devil horns, crowns, or decorative border. The icon must be recognizable when cropped to a small circle. Preserve generous negative space but do not make the mark so small that it disappears. Current image-generation iteration is a direction, not a final approved vector master.

## Post design
Default: square 1:1 canvas, black (#000000 or near-black), white or slightly off-white text, readable margins, expressive paragraph breaks. No glossy gradients, motivational stock photography, heavy logo, unnecessary illustration, badges, or clutter. No in-post hashtag block. Do not stylize the words more than necessary.

**Do not automatically impose a visible match, red accent, footer, numbering, or watermark on every post.** Simplicity is the competitive advantage. If any of these are later adopted, test them without compromising readability.

## Typography
Unpretentious, strongly legible, weight and size chosen for real phone screens. The prose sets the hierarchy. Avoid forced all-caps or theatrical emphasis. Short posts can have more negative space. Longer posts need generous leading and sufficient margins.

## Rendering and safe-area guidance
Start with 1080 x 1080 square assets because that is the primary concept. Verify current Instagram display behavior before automating layouts. Render each post and visually review long lines, paragraph breaks, sizing, crop, and contrast. Black squares are the output, not evidence of low editorial ambition.

## Independence of literature
Copy must work read aloud or pasted as plain text. The visual layer must never rescue weak words.

# Workflow | Learn from Published Work

## Principle
Editorial judgment predicts promise. Platform performance provides imperfect evidence. Do not confuse the two. Never fabricate "winning formulas" before real posts and real analytics exist.

## Data to capture after publication
Where available, store:
- Canonical post text and permalink, publication date and time.
- Reach/impressions and time window observed.
- Likes, shares/sends, saves, comments.
- Profile visits and follows attributable or observed with caveats.
- Followers versus non-followers reached, if available.
- A qualitative note about unexpected audience response.
- Any paid distribution or other unusual context.

Useful *candidate* comparisons include shares per reached account, saves per reached account, profile activity per reach, and follows per non-follower reach. Use consistent time windows, and do not compare a new 2-hour post with a 30-day old one as though equivalent.

## Learn carefully
- One breakout does not prove a permanent mechanism.
- The biggest post is not necessarily the best writing.
- Comments may reflect controversy more than healthy resonance.
- Topic, format, time, account size, distribution, and external reposts confound performance.
- Avoid overfitting to a single phrasing trick or crueler emotional angle.

## Monthly editorial review
1. Identify standout posts, underperformers, and surprises.
2. Read the copy before looking at metrics. Record literary judgment.
3. Compare results and reflect on why a mechanism may have worked.
4. Note counterexamples that challenge the theory.
5. Propose 2 to 4 concrete experiments, changing one major variable where practicable.
6. Preserve diversity; performance learning should deepen the voice, not flatten it.

## Gold Standards admission protocol
**Do not edit standards/gold-standards.md unless the owner explicitly requests a published example or enables an owner-configured automatic promotion rule in the console.** Preserve exact published text and observed metrics; never promote drafts or infer missing data.

For each owner-authorized inclusion, record:
- Exact published text, with original line breaks.
- Published post permalink and date.
- Metric snapshot with collection date and account context.
- Owner's reason for selection.
- Emotional mechanism and literary analysis.
- What may be worth emulating.
- What must not be copied mechanically.
- Caveats and alternative explanations.

Gold should include positive examples *because they actually broke out*, not because the AI scored them 30/30 or guessed they would. The Gold Standards document remains empty until then.

## Updating doctrine
Propose edits grounded in multiple observations. Let the owner approve doctrine changes. Save the original insight and the evidence, not just an algorithmically optimized phrase.
